I build the infrastructure that lets teams ship without breaking production.

07 / Working together

The practical details.

The things that actually determine whether a working relationship succeeds.

Timezone

Based in Pakistan, UTC+5. I keep 4 to 6 hours of overlap with the UK and 3 to 5 hours with US Eastern. I routinely work until 2am PKT to cover US mornings, and I have done this for years.

Company fit

Best fit is Seed to Series B, roughly 10 to 200 people, remote-first, with a real production workload. Teams that have infrastructure but no dedicated person owning it. Teams that need someone senior who can operate without being managed.

Culture fit

I work well in async, written-first teams. I document by default. I prefer blameless post-mortems over blame. I ask questions early rather than guessing. I am direct without being difficult.

Startup fit

I am comfortable with ambiguity. I have been the only technical person in the room more than once. I can go from "what do you actually need" to shipped and monitored without a lot of ceremony. I wear multiple hats without complaining.

How I engage

I start with a 30 day paid sprint so you can see real output before committing to anything long term. No long contracts, no retainer lock-in, no surprises. If it works, we continue. If it does not, you keep the work.

What I avoid

Environments where infrastructure is an afterthought. Reactive on-call with no runbooks. Approval chains that take days for a one line change. Roles that are purely frontend or purely ticket-driven.
